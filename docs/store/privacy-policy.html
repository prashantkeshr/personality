Privacy Policy – Personality

Effective 21 October 2026 (updated for the AI coach) · App by dhurta.Inc · Package com.dhurta.personality

Personality is a wellness, style and personal-growth app. It is built so that your personal information stays on your phone. We do not run servers that receive your data, we do not have accounts, and we do not sell or share personal data.

1. What the app stores, and where

  • Everything you enter or measure (profile, goals, weight and body records, water, meals, sleep, activity, habits, routines, posture and face-shape results, wardrobe, outfits and progress photos you choose to save) is stored only on your device, in an encrypted database.
  • Camera analysis (posture, exercise tracking, face shape, try-on) runs on your phone. Camera frames are processed in memory and discarded; nothing is recorded or uploaded unless you explicitly save a photo, and saved photos stay on your device.
  • App lock: if you set a PIN it is stored as a salted hash on your device; fingerprint and screen-lock checks are handled by Android, and the app never sees your biometric data.

2. Data from your phone

  • Step counter and physical activity (with the Physical activity permission) are read on the device to show steps and active minutes.
  • Health Connect (only if you connect it): the app may read steps, distance, exercise, sleep, weight, height and hydration to show them in the app. It does not write to Health Connect, and this data is never sent off the device or used for advertising. Use of information received from Health Connect follows the Health Connect permissions policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

3. Internet use

The app uses the internet only to download optional files when you tap Download:

  • Content packs (extra exercises, illustrations, style trends) from our public content library hosted on GitHub.
  • The AI coach's language model (Qwen2.5 0.5B Instruct, Apache-2.0, about 430 MB) from Hugging Face. After this one download the coach runs entirely on your phone; your questions and answers never leave the device.

Like any web request, GitHub and Hugging Face receive your IP address and basic request details to deliver the file; the app sends nothing about you, your health or your use of the app. Every download is checked with a checksum before use. Your health and personal data never use the internet.

4. What we do not do

  • No account, sign-in or cloud sync.
  • No advertising, ad identifiers or tracking.
  • No analytics or crash-reporting services. (Google ML Kit runs fully on the device; its usage-logging components are removed from the app.)
  • No sale or sharing of personal data with anyone.

5. Backups and exports

You can create an encrypted backup file or export your data (CSV/JSON). These files are created only when you ask, protected by a password you choose for backups, and saved or shared only where you pick. Android's own cloud backup of the app's data is turned off.

6. Notifications

Reminders are scheduled locally on your phone. You can choose their sound, vibration, lock-screen privacy and quiet hours, or turn them off.

7. Your control

  • View, edit or delete any record in the app.
  • Remove downloaded packs at any time in Settings → Content packs.
  • Revoke permissions (camera, physical activity, notifications, Health Connect) in Android settings at any time.
  • Uninstalling the app deletes all of its data from your phone.

8. Health information

Personality offers general wellness and style guidance. It is not a medical device and does not provide diagnosis or treatment. Talk to a qualified professional about medical questions.

9. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from them. Because data stays on the device, we hold no data about any user.

10. Changes

If this policy changes we will update this page and the effective date above. Material changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
